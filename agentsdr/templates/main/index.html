Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AgentSDR

help_outline How does AI-powered calling work?

Our AI calling system uses Bolna AI and advanced natural language processing to conduct human-like phone conversations. The AI can understand context, answer questions, qualify leads, and schedule appointments based on your custom scripts and rules. All calls are recorded and transcribed for your review.

help_outline Which CRM systems do you integrate with?

AgentSDR offers native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, and many other major CRM platforms. We also provide API access for custom integrations. All data syncs bidirectionally in real-time.

help_outline Is there a free trial available?

Yes! All plans include a 14-day free trial with full access to features. No credit card required to start your trial. You can cancel anytime during the trial period without being charged.

help_outline How secure is my data?

Security is our top priority. We use enterprise-grade encryption (AES-256) for data at rest and in transit. All data is stored on Supabase with row-level security. We're SOC 2 Type II compliant and GDPR ready. Your data is never shared with third parties.

help_outline Can I customize the AI calling scripts?

Absolutely! You have full control over your AI agent's conversation flow, responses, and behavior. You can create multiple scripts for different campaigns, A/B test variations, and continuously optimize based on performance data.

help_outline What kind of support do you provide?

Starter plans include email support with 24-hour response time. Professional plans get priority support with live chat. Enterprise customers receive a dedicated account manager, 24/7 phone support, and custom onboarding assistance.

help_outline Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, you can change your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll get immediate access to new features. When downgrading, changes take effect at your next billing cycle. No penalties or hidden fees.

help_outline How long does it take to get started?

Most teams are up and running within 5-10 minutes. Simply connect your CRM, configure your AI agent settings, and launch your first campaign. We provide guided onboarding and template libraries to help you get started quickly.
